Full-Service HVAC Care for San Diego Homes and Businesses

Heating and cooling around here covers a lot of ground: older gas furnaces in established neighborhoods, newer heat pumps in recent construction, ductless mini-splits in additions and converted garages, and packaged units on commercial buildings. Our team handles all of it. We have years of field experience, and we know how the local climate, the housing stock, and code requirements affect the way a system performs over time.

Most homeowners we meet need one of three things: a repair, a replacement, or regular maintenance. Repairs are usually quick: a burned contactor, a dirty flame sensor, a slow refrigerant leak, or a worn-out blower motor. Replacements get a proper load calculation, not a guess based on the old unit's tonnage, which keeps temperatures even and stops the new equipment from short-cycling itself to an early death. Maintenance visits include coil cleaning, a refrigerant charge check, safety control testing, and a static pressure reading, which does more than anything else to extend the life of your system.

Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in San Diego

Most mid-season breakdowns start with skipped maintenance, and the habits that prevent them are simple. Swap your filter every one to three months, keep plants and shrubs at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and rinse the condenser coil once a year in spring. Then book a professional tune-up twice a year, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

How old is your system? Its age tells you a lot. With regular service, most air conditioners reach 12 to 15 years, gas furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is past that point and also shows higher bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or repeated repairs, it's a strong candidate for a planned replacement, which is almost always cheaper and less stressful than an emergency replacement when the first heat wave hits.

The earlier you report a comfort problem, the cheaper it usually is. A capacitor that's starting to fail costs little to replace, yet the same part, neglected through a full season, can ruin the compressor and force a full system replacement. Save our number in your contacts now so help is one call away as soon as something sounds or feels wrong in your home. Early intervention saves time, money, and miserable nights.
